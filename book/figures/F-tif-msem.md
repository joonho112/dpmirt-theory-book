Test information (left) and the local standard error it implies (right), for a 20-item Rasch test with difficulties evenly spaced on $[-1.6, 1.6]$ (tick marks). Information is additive over items and peaks where the items sit; the standard error is the reciprocal square root and rises steeply outside the range the items cover. The two horizontal lines average the right-hand curve against a standard normal $G$: RMSEM averages on the variance scale and then takes a root, the mean standard error averages on the standard-error scale, and the first exceeds the second by @prp-msem-jensen.
