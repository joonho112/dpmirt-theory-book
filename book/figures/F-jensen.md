The three marginal reliability functionals of @sec-ch08-functionals, over Rasch tests of 5 to 95 items with difficulties evenly spaced on $[-1.6,1.6]$. Left: the two functionals plotted as distances above $\bar w$, so the sandwich is visible; $\rho_\Theta$ lies strictly between the other two everywhere, and the inner gap is roughly half the outer one. Note the vertical scale. Right: the outer gap $\tilde\rho-\bar w$ under four latent distributions, each standardized to variance 1. Heavy tails widen the gap and bimodality narrows it relative to the normal, because the gap tracks how much information varies over the mass of $G$, not how far $G$ is from normal. Every gap here is below 0.03. Deterministic quadrature; no simulation. Generated by code/R/09-figures.R.
