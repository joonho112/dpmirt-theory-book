Two Bayesian descendants of the finite-mixture route make two different component counts explicit. A Dirichlet process has infinitely many population atoms; its posterior count $K_J$ is the number occupied by the observed $J$ draws and therefore depends on sample size as well as the chosen concentration $\alpha$ (@sec-ch15). A mixture of finite mixtures instead places a prior directly on the finite population component count $K$ (Miller and Harrison 2018). Layout only; no analysis output appears.
