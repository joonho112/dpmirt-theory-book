The shrinkage weight $w_p=\sigma^2_\theta/(\sigma^2_\theta+se(\hat\theta_p)^2)$ against the conditional error variance, at four values of the between-person variance. The weight is the fraction of a person's estimate that comes from their own responses rather than from the population. It falls with measurement error and rises with between-person spread, which is why reliability is a property of a test *in a population* and not of a test. Generated by code/R/09-figures.R.
