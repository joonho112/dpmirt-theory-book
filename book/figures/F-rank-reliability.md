Deterministic pairwise rank concordance for a common-item Rasch test under four full-support latent distributions standardized to variance 1; score ties receive half credit. **Left:** each shape's concordance is plotted against its own $\bar w$ over tests from 6 through 180 items. Reliability is the leading driver, but shape does not drop out. **Right:** the cross-shape spread is calculated only after interpolation to a common $\bar w$ grid; it reaches a finite-range peak and then declines, consistent with concordance tending to one for every continuous shape as information grows. The heavy-tailed curve is a unit-variance logistic distribution, not a truncated $t$. Midpoint-quantile quadrature uses 800 nodes and is checked against 400 nodes; numerical summaries and convergence receipts are frozen under tables/F-rank-reliability-*.rds.
