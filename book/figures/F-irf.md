Item response functions (left) and item information (right) for four items under the 2PL. Difficulty $\beta$ shifts the curve horizontally; discrimination $\lambda$ sets its slope at $\theta=\beta$, which is $\lambda/4$. The Rasch model is $\lambda\equiv1$ (blue). Item information $\lambda_i^2\pi_{pi}(1-\pi_{pi})$ peaks at $\theta=\beta_i$ with height $\lambda_i^2/4$: a high-discrimination item separates sharply near its difficulty and says little elsewhere, a low-discrimination item is mildly informative over a wide range.
