**The triple-goal construction in two moves.** (a) The estimated distribution is reduced to K mass points at its midpoint quantiles, so the reported ensemble carries the estimated shape by construction. (b) Each person keeps the rank estimated from the posterior and is assigned the mass point that rank selects. Every arrow starts at a posterior mean and its arrowhead ends at the triple-goal estimate: right-pointing arrows have GR greater than PM, and left-pointing arrows have GR less than PM. The mapping stretches the compressed ensemble back onto the distribution's support without reordering anyone. Worked on a seeded twelve-person illustration under the constant-error working model of @sec-ch11, with the estimated distribution taken to be the generating mixture for display; nothing quantitative rests on it.
