The sum-score fallacy, computed exactly. **Top:** a standard normal latent distribution, and the sum-score distribution it produces on five items of difficulty $-1.5$ — skewness $-0.99$ with $38\%$ of respondents at the ceiling. A markedly non-normal score distribution from an exactly normal $G$. **Bottom:** a sharply bimodal $G$ (standard normal dashed for reference), and the sum-score distribution it produces on six items spread over $[-2.5, 2.5]$ — symmetric, single-peaked, and almost indistinguishable from what the normal $G$ gives on the same test (diamonds); the two differ by $0.035$ in total variation. Sum-score probabilities are exact: $\Pr(X=k\mid\theta)$ by the Poisson-binomial recursion, integrated over $G$ by quadrature. No simulation. Generated by code/R/09-figures.R.
